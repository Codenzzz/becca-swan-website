Redwood Pack Saga

Deliverance

Katya always believed in making her own destiny… until destiny came looking for her.

Bound by a childhood pact to mate with her best friend Jake if they hadn’t found their fated mates by their twenty-fifth birthday, they'd choose each other. Katya thought she had her future figured out.

But on the night of their mating ceremony, everything changes, forcing her to leave the only life she’s ever known behind.

A long-buried truth awakens in her blood, a dormant power tied to an ancient prophecy, and a child the darkness wants to destroy.

Hunted by a ruthless Order and drawn into a realm of fae, wolves and ancient magic.

With the future of the fae and werewolf kingdom at stake, and the enemy closing in, Katya must unlock the power within her, and rise as the warrior fate never warned her about.
