Christmas at the Mercantile

Emily Sinclair knows how to write a happy ending. Finding one of her own might be another matter.

Bestselling romance author Emily Sinclair never expected to return to Willow Creek under such heartbreaking circumstances. After losing her beloved grandmother, she inherits the family's old mercantile, a once-thriving general store filled with childhood memories.

Her plan is simple: sort through her grandmother's belongings, prepare the property for sale, and return to the life she's built away from her hometown.

But with Christmas approaching, Emily finds herself caught up in the traditions she grew up loving. As she begins bringing the neglected mercantile back to life, she also reconnects with Grayson Bennett, a handsome local rancher she remembers from her school days.

Grayson has built a life for himself in Willow Creek, and spending time with him reminds Emily of everything she loved about growing up there. Between Christmas celebrations, familiar faces, and an unexpected attraction, her plans to leave no longer seem quite so straightforward.

With her career waiting and the mercantile's future still undecided, Emily must work out what she really wants. And whether there's room in her life for a little Christmas romance of her own.

Christmas at the Mercantile is a heartwarming, kisses-only small-town Christmas romance filled with festive traditions, family memories, a charming rancher, and the magic of coming home. Part of the A Fireside Christmas shared-world series.
