About

Meet Becca Swan

Becca Swan writes stories about strong women, complicated love, and finding strength when everything feels lost. From wolves, shifters, and fae to small towns, second chances, and Christmas romance, her stories may cross different worlds, but they all have one thing in common: characters worth rooting for.

Expect romance, danger, twists, heartbreak, and plenty of moments where it’s always darkest before the dawn.

Story World

There’s always another story waiting.

I’ve never wanted to stay inside one genre. My stories can take you into a world of wolves, fae, fated mates, and paranormal romance, then straight into a psychological thriller, a dystopian world, or something much darker.

Other times, I want to take you somewhere completely different. A summer escape in The Bach, a small-town romance, or one of the Christmas stories I can’t seem to stop writing.

I’m even stepping into horror with The Family.

Some of my stories are published, while others are unfolding chapter by chapter across various reading platforms. My Inkitt subscribers get access to ongoing stories, new chapters, subscriber-only content, and more of what I’m currently writing.

Whatever world you step into, expect emotion, twists, memorable characters, and a story that keeps you turning the pages.
